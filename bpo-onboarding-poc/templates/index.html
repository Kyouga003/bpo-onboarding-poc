Automation & Compliance Coverage

  • Team onboarding: creates network access, assigns a Webex space per team/intake, and provisions collaboration access.
  • Secure remote access: remote agents receive VPN + Zero Trust policy status; on-site agents receive controlled LAN access.

  • Monitoring: latency, jitter, packet loss, and MOS samples can be refreshed per agent.
  • Audit logs: onboarding, updates, monitoring, rejections, and offboarding are recorded with timestamps and actor.
